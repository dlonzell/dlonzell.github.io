Presence and learning in classroom VR

With Cyan DeVeaux and Jeremy Bailenson, I’m investigating classroom VR through interviews, observations, and a comparison of group and solo 360° video experiences.

Ongoing research · Virtual reality · Classroom learning

Research focus

Virtual reality provides a way to place learners within a shared topic or situation. Its use in a classroom also raises questions about the relationship between immersion, social experience, and learning.

This project examines how learners experience classroom VR and how group and solo use compare. It brings my interest in meaningful learning experiences into a classroom setting.

Study approach

  • Interviews and observations

    Qualitative work examines learners’ experiences of using VR in the classroom.

  • Group and solo experiences

    A 62-student comparison investigates group versus solo 360° video experiences, with a focus on presence and learning.

  • Ongoing analysis

    Quantitative analysis and manuscript development are in progress. Findings will be added as the work develops.
